Quiz Night Community Centre

Trivia night, sorted.

One screen runs the game. Everybody else answers from their own phone.

Shown at the top of the big screen.

Set the game up on a laptop and point it at the big screen. You get a four-letter code for players to type in, and a live scoreboard to project all evening.

  • 125 questions across five rounds — Geography, History, General Knowledge, Celebrities and Lebanon
  • A fun fact after every answer, ready for the host to read out
  • Faster answers score more, so ties break themselves

Nothing needs installing on the players' phones.

Runs on the venue's own Wi-Fi. No accounts, no app store, and nothing leaves the building.
